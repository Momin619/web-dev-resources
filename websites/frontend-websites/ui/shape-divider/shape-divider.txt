🌀 Shape Dividers – Seamless Section Transitions for Your Website
Shape Dividers is a powerful and easy-to-use tool that helps you generate beautiful, customizable SVG shape transitions between sections of your website. Whether you're building a portfolio, landing page, or blog, Shape Dividers adds a modern, flowing aesthetic without the need for design software or complex coding.

📁 What You Can Do:

Generate SVG-based section dividers with live previews

Choose from a variety of shapes: waves, curves, triangles, and more

Customize colors, height, flip, and orientation

Copy CSS & HTML snippets instantly

Export clean SVG code ready to paste into your project

💡 Purpose:
Shape Dividers helps front-end developers and designers break the monotony of rigid layouts by adding organic, smooth transitions that improve visual flow and hierarchy across sections.

🛠 Ideal For:

Developers building engaging landing pages

Designers enhancing page aesthetics with ease

Creators looking to avoid image-heavy layouts

Quick prototypes or full production websites

✅ How to Use:

Visit ShapeDividers.com

Select a shape from the library

Customize its size, position, color, and direction

Copy the auto-generated HTML/CSS or SVG code

Paste it into your project and enjoy the transformation
