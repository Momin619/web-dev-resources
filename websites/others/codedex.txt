📁 What You Can Do:

Learn Python, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and more

Complete quests, earn XP, and unlock achievements

Practice coding directly in the browser

Track progress through personalized dashboards

Join a supportive, gamified learning community

Codedex makes coding accessible and exciting by combining education with storytelling and gamification—perfect for students, hobbyists, and visual learners who find traditional tutorials dull.

🛠 Ideal For:

Absolute beginners starting their coding journey

Teens and young adults who enjoy game-like learning

Self-learners looking for a fun, structured path

Teachers and mentors introducing code in a playful way
